## Scope and authority

The observed corpus comprises two coauthored papers: Macdonald and Gulbudak's 2023 NPZD mathematical-ecology paper, read in its author revision/source and arXiv version, and Macdonald, Blanco-Portillo, Feldman and Ram's 2024 cultural-network paper, read in the published main text and attached SI. Main prose, methods, empirical interpretation and mathematical appendix examples were inspected, rather than abstracts alone. Different versions of NPZD count as one paper. This is a representative cross-genre sample, not a survey of the author's complete output.

The issue-376 pass added direct inspection of the EHS author TeX, Methods lines 187–276, against the published main/SI and re-read complete selected NPZD arguments with their setup, definitions and cited-theorem applications. It did not extend the corpus to other biological articles or read their cached texts. The new Methods/proof observations identify exact passages and acknowledge omissions or discrepancies rather than treating source prose as a complete methodological or mathematical audit. Bibliographic verification inherited from version 1 is not a new publisher-access claim.

The EHS contribution statement credits JCM with the original draft and all authors with review/editing. Neither that statement nor the two-author NPZD byline identifies who wrote each sentence or proof. Treat recurring patterns as features of these coauthored papers that may help reproduce the requested voice. Do not describe measured rates as uniquely personal preferences.

## Applying the package

1. Read all applicable components and the project's own instructions before
   drafting or reviewing. State its readership, scientific question, current
   claim scope and submission format. Preserve unrelated local instructions.
2. Keep observed coauthored patterns, explicit author preferences and audience
   adaptations distinct. Author instructions and verified house style control;
   descriptive punctuation/pronoun/sentence statistics are never quotas.
3. Explain the actual operation, target and reason through the evidence. Define
   quantities before use and cite adopted methods, data, protocols and software
   where used in Methods. Preserve exploratory/confirmatory status, adverse
   outcomes, pending work, undefined cases and uncertainty.
4. Review every complete mathematical statement, proof and interpretation,
   including setup, domains, binder order/dependence, boundary cases, cited
   hypotheses and downstream summaries. Use symbolic, numerical and pinned
   Lean checks within their real coverage; a conditional component result does
   not certify a full fitted pipeline or stochastic limit.
5. Present original visual operations and literal approved scientific values.
   Put schematic status in captions, keep full precision in source artifacts,
   and review actual colour/grayscale figures with complete captions after
   integration at the intended width. Figure styling admits no new estimator,
   scientific fit, environment upgrade or study endpoint.
6. Re-audit the complete main text and SI independently, including generated
   inputs, tables, captions and availability statements. Inspect source-matched
   PDFs and run the applicable project checks. Obtain separate independent
   manuscript review for scientific meaning, proof completeness, attribution,
   author voice and presentation; resolve findings before calling the pass
   complete. Guidance approval cannot satisfy that application gate.

Existing claim/proof bindings protect provenance rather than incomplete prose.
A warranted bound-content change needs the project's prospective semantic
review and scope-version process. Retain earlier scopes, failed attempts,
ledgers and receipts; never overwrite historical hashes merely to clear a
check. No scientific run or binding change belongs to guidance preparation or
installation itself.

## Review and handoff

The first gate reviews this complete six-file candidate, its source/read limits,
change record and proposed rollout policy before application or installation.
Earlier version approvals cannot be carried forward. Challenge observation
versus requirement labels, exclusions, scientific scope, complete-proof
instructions, visual access claims and preservation safeguards. Mark it
reviewed only with an actual independent record tied to the exact files.

After that gate, the manuscript agent inventories first uses, definitions and
citation-at-use separately in main Methods and SI; reads every complete proof
with binder/dependency and TeX–Lean correspondence records; reviews the complete
main/SI/figures; runs checks appropriate to changed sources; and obtains the
separate independent application review. State exactly what was read, checked,
built, visually inspected and left unresolved. A successful build, preliminary
inventory or guidance review establishes no manuscript's scientific completion
or submission readiness.
